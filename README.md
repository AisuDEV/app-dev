# World Trigger
**World Trigger** is one of my favorite anime series.  
It’s a **sci-fi action** anime filled with **strategic battles**, teamwork, and diverse characters.
---
## Overview
**Japanese Title:** ワールドトリガー (Wārudo Torigā)  
**Genre:** Action, Sci-Fi, Supernatural  
**Author:** Daisuke Ashihara  
**Studio:** Toei Animation  
> “The difference in strength can be overcome by strategy and teamwork.”
---
## Plot Summary
When mysterious gates to another world suddenly appear in Mikado City, monstrous beings known as **Neighbors** emerge and wreak havoc.  
To fight back, an organization called **Border** is formed — using alien technology called **Triggers**.
---
## What Makes It Special
- **Tactical Team Fights** – Every battle feels like a chess match.  
- **Large Cast of Characters** – Each with unique abilities and personalities.  
- **World-Building** – The Neighbor Worlds and Trigger systems are deep and interesting.  
- **No “main character plot armor”** – Wins are earned through strategy.
---
## Favorite Characters
1. **Osamu Mikumo** – The underdog strategist  
2. **Yūma Kuga** – The cheerful but deadly Neighbor  
3. **Chika Amatori** – The shy sniper with massive power  
4. **Jin Yūichi** – The laid-back future-seer
